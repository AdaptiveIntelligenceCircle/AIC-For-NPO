# AIC-For-NPO

**Adaptation layer of Adaptive Intelligence Circle principles and tools for Non-Profit Organizations.**

> This is not a product.  
> This is not a ready-to-deploy solution.  
> This is a careful adaptation of AIC’s core principles (Ethical-from-kernel, Third Path, Zero-donation, Human Meaning) for use by non-profit organizations that share similar values of independence, transparency, and long-term responsibility.

**Status:** Early design / documentation stage (pre-implementation)  
**Parent project:** [Adaptive Intelligence Circle](https://github.com/AdaptiveIntelligenceCircle)  
**Related:** Parallel TestNet (Phase 0 still in progress)

---

## Purpose

AIC-For-NPO exists to explore how the foundational ideas of AIC can be useful for non-profit organizations that:

- Want stronger internal governance and decision transparency
- Wish to reduce the risk of mission drift caused by funding or external pressure
- Care about human meaning and ethical constraints in their use of AI and coordination tools
- Prefer independence from both pure state control and pure commercial capture

It is **not** intended to turn AIC into a service provider for NPOs, nor to create dependency.

---

## Core Principles (inherited & adapted)

| AIC Principle              | Adaptation for NPOs                                      |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Ethical-from-kernel       | Critical decisions should have clear, fail-closed ethical checks near the point of authorization |
| Third Path                | Avoid structures that make the organization easily capturable by large funders or political agendas |
| Zero-donation (spirit)    | Prefer funding and partnerships that do not create hidden control or personal benefit |
| Human Meaning             | Protect spaces where human judgment and meaning-making remain central |
| Transparency              | Maintain inspectable decision logs and governance artifacts |
| Under-claim               | Do not over-promise what the tools can do |

## What this repository contains

- Mapping of AIC principles to non-profit governance needs
- Eligibility considerations (to protect the integrity of Third Path)
- Lightweight governance adaptation ideas
- Templates for decision logging and human-gating
- Notes on how this can later connect to Parallel TestNet
- Clear boundaries of what this project will **not** do

---

## What this repository is NOT

- A complete software product
- A funding or fiscal sponsorship service
- A replacement for existing NPO governance frameworks
- A way for AIC to accept restricted funding through intermediaries
- A growth or marketing initiative

---

## Relationship to Parallel TestNet

Any concrete tools developed here should eventually be testable against the Ethical Kernel and governance mechanisms of Parallel TestNet.  
Until Parallel TestNet Phase 0 has clearer runnable artifacts, this repository remains primarily documentation and design.
