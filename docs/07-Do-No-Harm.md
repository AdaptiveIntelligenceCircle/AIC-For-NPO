# Do No Harm & Non-Capture

## Core commitments

AIC-For-NPO is guided by the following constraints:

1. **Non-Capture**  
   The ideas and any future tools associated with this repository must not become instruments of hidden influence by funders, governments, political movements, or commercial entities.

2. **Human Responsibility**  
   Important organizational decisions must remain under meaningful human authority. Automation and AI assistance are secondary and must not displace ethical judgment.

3. **No Dependency Creation**  
   This work must not create reliance on AIC as a service provider, brand, or gatekeeper of legitimacy.

4. **Inspectability**  
   Principles, templates, and any future code should remain open to examination.

5. **Fail-closed preference**  
   Where authorization or high-stakes decisions are involved, the default posture should favor refusal or human review when confidence is low.

## Practical implications

- Funding relationships of organizations using these ideas should be examined for influence risk.
- AI tools used in governance contexts should be treated as advisory, not authoritative.
- Public association with AIC should accurately reflect the limited, experimental nature of this work.
- Any observed tension between convenience and independence should be resolved in favor of independence.

## Relationship to broader AIC principles

These constraints are direct extensions of:
- Third Path
- Ethical-from-kernel
- Zero-donation spirit
- Under-claim
- Human Meaning

They are not optional add-ons.
