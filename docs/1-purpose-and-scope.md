# 01 – Purpose and Scope

## Why AIC-For-NPO exists

Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) is itself a non-profit effort.  
Many of the problems AIC tries to address at the infrastructure level (capture, loss of meaning, opaque decision-making, short-term pressure) also appear inside non-profit organizations.

AIC-For-NPO is an attempt to translate the core ideas of AIC into forms that may be useful for other non-profits, without turning AIC into a conventional service organization.

## Primary goals

1. Help non-profits that value independence think more clearly about governance, funding relationships, and the use of AI/coordination tools.
2. Provide lightweight, transparent patterns (decision logs, human-gating points, eligibility self-checks) inspired by Ethical-from-kernel and Third Path.
3. Create a bridge so that future runnable components from Parallel TestNet can be tested in real non-profit decision contexts.
4. Keep the barrier to understanding low while keeping the barrier to misuse high.

## Explicit non-goals

- Becoming a fiscal host or grant intermediary
- Providing managed services
- Helping organizations optimize for growth or fundraising at the expense of mission integrity
- Creating dependency on AIC
- Diluting Zero-donation or Third Path principles

## Scope boundaries

This repository focuses on **design, documentation, templates, and eventual lightweight prototypes**.  
It does not currently offer production-ready software.

Any future software components must remain compatible with the Ethical Kernel direction of the main AIC project and should be exerciseable on Parallel TestNet.
