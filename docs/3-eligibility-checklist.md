# 03 – Eligibility & Self-Assessment Checklist

This checklist is not a formal certification.  
It is a tool for organizations (and for AIC maintainers) to reflect on whether engagement with AIC-For-NPO ideas is likely to remain compatible with Third Path and non-capture principles.

## Minimum reflective questions

An organization considering using or adapting material from this repository should be able to answer honestly:

1. **Mission clarity**  
   Do we have a clearly stated purpose that is not primarily about organizational survival or growth?

2. **Independence**  
   Are we currently free enough from any single funder, government, or commercial partner that we can still refuse significant requests?

3. **Funding transparency**  
   Are we willing to be open about major funding sources and the conditions attached to them?

4. **Decision integrity**  
   Do we have (or are we willing to create) basic records of why important decisions were made?

5. **Attitude toward capture**  
   Do we treat the risk of being captured (by money, politics, or prestige) as a real and ongoing concern?

6. **Human judgment**  
   Are we committed to keeping human deliberation central on questions of purpose and ethics, rather than outsourcing them to tools or external pressures?

## Red flags (higher risk of misalignment)

- Heavy dependence on one or two large restricted grants that dictate program direction
- Leadership incentives strongly tied to rapid growth or external recognition
- Reluctance to document or explain major decisions
- Desire to use AIC branding primarily for fundraising or legitimacy without internal practice
- Expectation that AIC will provide ongoing operational support or funding access

## Note for AIC maintainers

Material in this repository should remain usable by organizations that score imperfectly on the above questions — provided they engage in good faith.  
However, active collaboration, joint announcements, or deeper technical integration should be approached with greater caution when multiple red flags are present.
