# Human-Gating Checklist (Template)

Use before finalizing decisions in categories that should remain under explicit human judgment.

**Decision under consideration:**  

**Category** (funding / partnership / mission change / public position / data handling / other):  

## Checklist

- [ ] Has the full context been presented to the people responsible for authorization?
- [ ] Have alternative options been genuinely considered (not only the preferred one)?
- [ ] Is there any external party that stands to gain significantly from one particular outcome?
- [ ] Does this decision reduce or increase our future ability to say “no”?
- [ ] Have we checked alignment with our stated mission and independence principles?
- [ ] Is there a clear record of who authorized this and why?
- [ ] Is a future review point appropriate?
